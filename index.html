About the Game

Tap Master: Reflex is a fast-paced reaction game. Tap the targets as quickly as possible, build your combo, earn points, and try to beat your high score.

  • 30-second gameplay
  • Three difficulty levels
  • Combo-based scoring
  • Personal high scores
  • Sound and haptic feedback

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play?

Tap the target as quickly as you can before it disappears. Keep tapping to build your combo and increase your score.

How can I get a higher score?

React quickly, avoid missing targets, and maintain a high combo.

How do I reset my high scores?

Open Settings inside the game and select "Reset High Scores".
